# Changelog

## 0.1.0-beta.1 (unreleased)

First public beta.

- Avro Phonetic typing in any app; Space always inserts what Avro would type.
- Suggestions after the exact conversion: dictionary words, conventional spellings (university
  acronyms, the 64 districts), and the word meant by informal spellings (`bari`: বাড়ি,
  `jekono`: যেকোন).
- Optional, off by default: "Suggest English loanwords (beta)" adds up to three known words for
  English and informal spellings (`computer`: কম্পিউটার) after the engine's own suggestions. Space is
  unchanged, and offensive words are never suggested.
- Personal dictionary, learned choices, backup and restore, all kept on your Mac.
- The Bornolipi app: setup assistant, status, repair, update check, diagnostics and uninstall.
- Universal (Apple silicon and Intel), macOS 13 or later. Not notarized: allow it once with Open
  Anyway (docs/INSTALL.md).
